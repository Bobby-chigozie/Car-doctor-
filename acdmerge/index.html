Safety and accuracy notice

This offline guide gives possible causes, not a confirmed diagnosis. Verify codes and specifications for the exact vehicle. If brakes, steering, oil pressure, fuel leaks, overheating, or severe engine shaking occur, stop safely and get qualified help. Never work under a vehicle supported only by a jack; disconnect power or depressurize systems only using the vehicle service procedure.
